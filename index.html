Jungle Treasure Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026 · App: Jungle Treasure (Android, package com.brickland.dash2)

In short: Jungle Treasure is a game made for players of all ages, including children. We do not ask for your name, email, age, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no chat and no way to share anything with other players. Your game progress stays on your device. The free version shows child-safe ads from Google AdMob; the optional Premium Pack removes them.

Who we are

Jungle Treasure is made by asy ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at devinnosoftnet@gmail.com.

Information we do not collect

We, the developer, do not collect, receive or store any personal information from players. The game has no sign-in, no player profiles, no chat, no friends list, no leaderboards, no camera or microphone use, and no location access. We do not run our own servers or analytics, and we do not sell or rent any data.

Game progress saved on your device

The game saves your progress in a small file inside the app's private storage on your device. It holds things like your high score, unlocked levels, stars, lives, the sound setting, ad-reward counters and whether the Premium Pack is owned. This file never leaves your device: we cannot see it, and it is not backed up to the cloud by the app. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android Settings, deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows two kinds of full-screen ads, served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC:

  • Rewarded ads, which a player chooses to watch for an in-game reward (for example an extra life or a healing herb).
  • Interstitial ads, shown occasionally after a game over or between levels.

Because children may play, every ad request from Jungle Treasure is set up for children:

  • Ad requests are marked as child-directed under the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (for the EU/UK GDPR).
  • Ads are limited to content rated "G" (suitable for general audiences).
  • Ads are not personalized: no interest-based advertising and no remarketing.
  • The app does not request permission to use the Android Advertising ID.

To deliver ads, measure that they were shown, limit how often they repeat and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends Google some technical information, such as your device's IP address, device model and operating system, app version, and how you interact with an ad. Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy and its rules for child-directed ad requests. We do not receive any of it in a way that identifies a player.

Premium Pack purchase (Google Play Billing)

The optional Premium Pack is a one-time purchase that removes ads and gives more lives when you continue. Before a purchase, the game asks a grown-up to answer a short question. The purchase itself is handled entirely by Google Play: payment details are entered into Google Play, never into the game, and we never see them. The game only asks Google Play whether the Premium Pack is owned, so it can turn on Premium and restore it after a reinstall. Google handles purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

Children's privacy

Jungle Treasure is suitable for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in their country). The only third-party services in the app are Google AdMob, which is configured for child-directed treatment as described above, and Google Play Billing. Purchases sit behind a grown-up check. Parents or guardians who have questions, or who want to ask about any information connected with their child's use of the game, can contact us at devinnosoftnet@gmail.com, and we will respond promptly.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no player data, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove the game's saved progress, uninstall the app or use Android Settings > Apps > Jungle Treasure > Storage > Clear data. Information that Google processes for ads or purchases is kept and deleted by Google under its own policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

Security

Communication between the app and Google's ad and billing services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Save data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or state laws in the US), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold any personal data, we usually have nothing to hand over, but you can always contact us and we will help, including with requests about data Google processes for the app.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches players. If a change ever needs consent, we will ask for it first.
