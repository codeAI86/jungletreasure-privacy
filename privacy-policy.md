# Privacy Policy for Jungle Treasure

_Last updated: 2026-11-08_

Jungle Treasure ("the game") is a jungle adventure game for players of all ages, including children. This policy explains what information the game handles. Contact: **devinnosoftnet@gmail.com**

## Your game progress stays on your device
- Your progress (high score, unlocked levels, stars, lives, sound setting and whether the Premium Pack is owned) is saved **only on your device**. We do not upload, copy, view or have access to it.
- The game has no account system, no sign-in, no chat and no servers of its own.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)
The free version shows ads using **Google AdMob** (rewarded ads you choose to watch for an extra life or a herb, and occasional full-screen ads after a game over or between levels).

Because children may play, every ad request is marked as **child-directed** and limited to **G-rated** ads. Ads are **not personalized**, and the game does not use the Android Advertising ID.

To serve and measure ads, Google AdMob may collect and use, from your device:
- IP address and approximate location derived from it
- Device and app information (device model, OS version, app version) and ad interaction/diagnostic data

We do not receive this data and do not combine it with anything else. How Google handles it is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

## Premium Pack (Google Play)
The optional Premium Pack is a one-time purchase that removes ads. A grown-up check is shown before buying. Payment is handled entirely by **Google Play**; we never see your payment details. The game only asks Google Play whether the pack is owned.

## Children
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
- The game follows the Google Play Families Policy.
- Parents can contact us at any time with questions.

## What we do not do
- We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
- We do not sell personal information.

## Permissions
The game does not request special permissions. It needs internet access only to load ads and to check the Premium Pack with Google Play.

## Data retention and deletion
Everything the game stores is on your device; uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it. We hold no data on our side to delete.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a new date.
